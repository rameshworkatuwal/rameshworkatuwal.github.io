3 Months in Dubai

Nov 2026

August 2, 2026

Just a simple date. And for me too, it was just another simple date — 108 days before today.

I didn't realize that day would carry so much weight in my heart.

At the time, I kept telling myself that I was probably just going away for a short holiday. Maybe I'd travel somewhere for a few days and come back. I tried not to think too much about it. I tried to convince myself that it wasn't a big deal.

But somehow, my eyes were quietly crying. Even I didn't understand why.

Until that day, I had never cried before leaving a place or going somewhere. But this time felt different. There was something inside me that I couldn't explain — a mixture of fear, excitement, uncertainty, and the feeling of leaving behind everything that had been familiar to me.

I still remember wrapping my arms around my bed and lying there for a minute or two, as if I was saying goodbye without actually saying goodbye. Haha.

I'll save that little story for another section.

For now, let me take you through my three-month journey. Let me take you right back to the airport — to the very beginning, and tell you everything that happened along the way.

So, here we go.
